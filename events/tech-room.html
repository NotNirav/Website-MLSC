About the event

Techroom is an MLSC initiative focused on strengthening students’ understanding of AI/ML, Web Development, and Competitive Programming. The session brought together learners through structured, mentor-led discussions and interactive learning experiences.

With an emphasis on hands-on practice, collaboration, and exposure to industry-grade tools and technologies, Techroom created an environment where students could explore concepts beyond the classroom and build confidence through practical learning.
