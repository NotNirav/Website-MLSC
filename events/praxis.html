About the event

Praxis is a multi-round technical competition designed to challenge participants across different areas of technology and problem-solving. The event brings together technical knowledge, web development, AI innovation, and competitive programming through a series of progressively challenging rounds.

Participants take on four competitive stages—Tech Quiz, Debug Sprint, AI Innovation Challenge, and Progressive Competitive Programming—putting their analytical thinking, technical expertise, creativity, and problem-solving skills to the test.

Tech Quiz

Test your technical knowledge in the opening challenge.

Debug Sprint

Find the flaws, fix the code, and think on your feet.

AI Innovation Challenge

Turn ideas into innovative solutions with AI.

Competitive Programming

Push your logic and coding skills through progressive challenges.
