About the event

BlueBit 2.0 is MLSC PCCOE’s flagship hackathon, bringing together passionate innovators, developers, and problem-solvers to turn ideas into impactful solutions. The hackathon provides a platform for participants to collaborate, experiment with technology, and tackle real-world challenges through creative thinking and hands-on development.

From ideation to implementation, BlueBit 2.0 encourages teams to transform their concepts into working solutions while learning, collaborating, and pushing the boundaries of what they can build.
