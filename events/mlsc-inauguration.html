About the event

The MLSC PCCOE Inauguration marked the beginning of a new chapter for the Microsoft Learn Student Community at PCCOE. The event brought together students, faculty, and the MLSC team to introduce the community, its vision, and the opportunities it aims to create through technology, learning, and collaboration.

It set the stage for a year of technical events, hands-on learning, competitions, and opportunities for students to connect and grow together.
