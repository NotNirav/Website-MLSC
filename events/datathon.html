About the event

Datathon is a data-focused challenge that brings together students to explore real-world problems through data, analysis, and innovative thinking. The event encourages participants to work with data, uncover meaningful insights, and develop solutions driven by evidence.

Through collaboration, analytical thinking, and problem-solving, participants get an opportunity to transform raw information into ideas that can create meaningful impact.

Data Exploration
